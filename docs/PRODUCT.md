# Product

NARRATIVE is a local, inspectable story engineering research harness for authors and narrative researchers. It represents one versioned story graph, simulates consequences, diagnoses dependencies, and evaluates isolated revisions. It does not certify literary merit.

The initial product is a Python 3.12+ library and CLI with offline demonstrations. Success means all 15 acceptance criteria in SPEC have executable examples or tests; contradictions are detected without paid calls; canon cannot change silently; reproducible experiment output includes actual costs and limitations. Subjective reader experience requires external calibration and remains unvalidated.
